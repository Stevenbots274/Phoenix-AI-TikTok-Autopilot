A small, useful cookie footprint.

Phoenix uses storage needed to keep you signed in and remember a small number of workspace preferences. It does not use advertising cookies.

Last updated: August 2026

Authentication cookie

When you sign in, Phoenix stores a cookie named phoenix_session. It is HttpOnly, scoped to the site, and used to keep your account session active. Without it, protected workspace pages cannot work.

Workspace preference

Phoenix stores the sidebar collapse preference in your browser's local storage under phoenix.sidebarCollapsed. This preference does not contain account content or credentials.

Third-party resources

The public interface loads fonts from Google Fonts. Connected providers such as TikTok, AI services, search services, hosting, and storage may have their own cookies and privacy practices when you visit or authorize them.

Your choices

You can clear site cookies and local storage through your browser settings. Clearing the authentication cookie signs you out; clearing the sidebar preference restores the default responsive layout.
